The problem

Verbosity is a hidden tax.

Raw $/MTok comparisons assume every model spends the same tokens on the same job. In reality, concise configurations finish a complex task in 3,000 – 19,000 output tokens while verbose ones burn 115,000 – 204,000 — up to a 65× swing that token-price charts never see.

A verbose model doesn’t just cost more per task — it drains the same monthly pool faster, quietly shrinking your plan’s real capacity.
The method

The task-adjusted formulation

Three numbers turn a raw token quota into purchasing power you can actually compare.

Monthly task capacity
Ntasks= TmonthlyCtask

How many real tasks your monthly token pool can actually solve.

Cost per task
Costtask= PmonthlyNtasks

What you actually pay for each completed task.

Tasks per dollar
Tasks$= 1Costtask

The true efficiency metric: completed tasks delivered per dollar.

T = monthly usable tokens · C = output tokens per task (DeepSWE v1.1 medians) · P = monthly fee.
All evaluated reasoning effort tiers (Low to Max) are included by default in the rankings.

Key takeaways

What the shifts reveal

The verbosity penalty

Big allowance ≠ real value

gemini-3.8-flash offers tens of billions of tokens and tops raw $/MTok charts — but at 120,488 median output tokens per task, its effective task cost falls behind far leaner models.

The coding workhorses

Concise models, huge pools

ChatGPT’s gpt-5.6-luna (7,918 tokens) and gpt-5.6-terra (11,453) pair huge pools with lean consumption — terra plans climb up to +41 spots once verbosity is priced in, and glm-5.3-flash breaks the top 10 on quota alone.

The coverage flip

Twice the map

DeepSWE ranks 22 model families with plans — nearly double CursorBench’s 11. DeepSeek V4, Kimi K3, GLM-5.2/5.3, Qwen3.8 Max, Opus 4.8/5, GPT-5.5–6 Astra, Gemini 3.x Flash and Grok 4.x all enter; claude-opus-5.5, gpt-6-sol, grok-4.7 and swe-2 now await evaluation.

Coverage gaps

Awaiting measurement

39 models 126 plans pending

These models from the real-api-pricing dataset have no DeepSWE v1.1 evaluation — their output tokens per task are unknown, so they’re flagged [Pending DeepSWE] and excluded from the ranking.

API-only rows are excluded by design

API-only plans for benchmarked models (e.g. “Claude Opus 5 API”) carry no monthly token quota, so they can’t be ranked as subscriptions — their absence is intentional, not a coverage gap.

How a pending model joins

When DeepSWE or a community saturation run publishes completion-token figures for Opus 5.5, GPT-6 Sol, Grok 4.7, MiMo v2.6, Step-5, SWE-2 or any other pending model: update data/model-token-consumption.json and re-run compute_task_ranking.py.

Unmetered promos are flagged, not ranked

Devin Pro × SWE-2 is unmetered (≈$0/MTok, unbounded quota) under a promotion running until 2026-10-31 — it carries a promo flag in the derived data rather than a rank, and must be re-verified when the promo ends.
