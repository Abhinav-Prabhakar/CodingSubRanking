The problem

Verbosity is a hidden tax.

Raw $/MTok comparisons assume every model spends the same tokens on the same job. In reality, concise configs finish a complex task in 5–10K output tokens while verbose ones burn 120–160K — a 15–20× swing that token-price charts never see.

Tokens consumed by the same workload

A verbose model doesn’t just cost more per task — it drains the same monthly pool faster, quietly shrinking your plan’s real capacity.
The method

The task-adjusted formulation

Three numbers turn a raw token quota into purchasing power you can actually compare.

Monthly task capacity
Ntasks= TmonthlyCtask

How many real tasks your monthly token pool can actually solve.

Cost per task
Costtask= PmonthlyNtasks

What you actually pay for each completed task.

Tasks per dollar
Tasks$= 1Costtask

The true efficiency metric: completed tasks delivered per dollar.

T = monthly usable tokens · C = output tokens per task (CursorBench 4.0) · P = monthly fee.
Baseline uses the Medium reasoning tier — switch tiers live in the rankings.

Key takeaways

What the shifts reveal

The verbosity penalty

Big allowance ≠ real value

gemini-3.8-flash offers tens of billions of tokens and tops raw $/MTok charts — but at 128,364 output tokens per task, its effective task cost falls behind far leaner models.

The coding workhorses

Concise models, huge pools

ChatGPT’s gpt-5.6-luna and gpt-5.6-terra pair big monthly pools with ~7,300–7,600 tokens per task for industry-leading yields, while composer-2.5 balances at 17,347 — 5,000+ tasks per dollar on Cursor Ultra.

Quality at moderate overhead

The premium sweet spot

Sonnet 5 (39,114 tokens) and Opus 5 (45,272 tokens) trade some efficiency for benchmark quality — climbing past more verbose competitors on effective cost per task.

Coverage gaps

Awaiting measurement

40 models 157 plans pending

These models from the real-api-pricing dataset have no CursorBench 4.0 evaluation — their output tokens per task are unknown, so they’re flagged [Pending CursorBench] and excluded from the ranking.

API-only rows are excluded by design

API-only plans for benchmarked models (e.g. “Claude Opus 5 API”) carry no monthly token quota, so they can’t be ranked as subscriptions — their absence is intentional, not a coverage gap.

How a pending model joins

When CursorBench or a community saturation run publishes completion-token figures for DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM, Qwen, StepFun or MiniMax: update data/model-token-consumption.json and re-run compute_task_ranking.py.
